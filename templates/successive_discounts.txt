Successive discounts are consecutive percentage reductions applied one after another.

Core Concept:
Discounts are NOT simply added together! A 20% discount followed by a 10% discount is NOT a 30% discount.

Formula:
  Final Price = Original Price × (1 - d₁) × (1 - d₂)
  Single Equivalent Discount = 1 - (1 - d₁) × (1 - d₂)

Example: An item listed at $100 has successive discounts of 20% and 10%.
  After 1st discount: 100 × (1 - 0.20) = $80.
  After 2nd discount: 80 × (1 - 0.10) = $72.
  Total discount = 100 - 72 = $28 (which is 28%, NOT 30%).

Shortcut:
  Equivalent Discount = d₁ + d₂ - (d₁ × d₂) / 100
  = 20 + 10 - (200 / 100) = 30 - 2 = 28%.

Tip: Successive discounts are always LESS than their direct numerical sum.
