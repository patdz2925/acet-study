Problem-to-Solution analogies pair an issue with its direct remedy or resolution.

Relationship: [SOLUTION] is used to relieve, cure, or eliminate [PROBLEM].

Strategy:
1. Bridge sentence: "[B] is applied to treat or eliminate [A]."
2. Keep the problem and remedy precise:
   - Thirst : Water (Water quenches thirst).
   - Pain : Analgesic (An analgesic relieves pain).
3. Confirm the order: Is the problem on the left and the solution on the right?

Example: INSOMNIA : SLEEP
  Bridge: Insomnia is the condition of being unable to sleep.
  Match: DROUGHT : RAIN (A drought is the condition of lacking rain).

Tip: Make sure the remedy directly solves the problem rather than merely masking or following it.
