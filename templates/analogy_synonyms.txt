Analogy Synonyms compare pairs where both terms share the same or highly similar meanings.

Relationship: [WORD A] means essentially the same thing as [WORD B].

Strategy:
1. Bridge sentence: "[A] is a synonym for [B]."
2. Check nuance and intensity:
   - Neutral synonyms: Huge : Large.
   - Elevated/literary synonyms: Melancholy : Sad.
   - Intensity analogies: Whimpering : Sobbing (mild to extreme).
3. If the first pair contains two formal or elevated words, the answer pair often follows the same register.

Example: METICULOUS : CAREFUL
  Bridge: Meticulous means extremely careful.
  Match: COURTEOUS : POLITE (Courteous means polite).

Tip: Eliminate choices where one word is an antonym or merely related by topic rather than meaning.
