Sentence Completion measures vocabulary and the ability to recognize context clues.

Key Rules & Strategy:
1. Identify Directional Clues:
   - Same direction (support): and, moreover, similarly, therefore, thus.
   - Opposite direction (contrast): although, but, however, despite, conversely.
2. Predict the missing word in your own mind before looking at the choices.
3. Eliminate options that don't fit the tone (positive, negative, neutral) of the sentence.
4. Plug your selected word back into the full sentence to confirm natural meaning.

Example: "Although the critic was usually ________, her review of the novel was surprisingly generous."
  Clue: "Although" signals contrast with "generous". The missing word must mean harsh or critical (e.g., caustic, acerbic, severe).

Tip: Read the complete sentence before reading choices. Don't let an impressive-sounding word distract you from the true context.
