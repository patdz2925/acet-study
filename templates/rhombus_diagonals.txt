Properties and geometric formulas for a rhombus.

Key Geometric Properties:
1. All 4 sides are equal in length (s).
2. Opposite angles are equal; adjacent angles are supplementary (add to 180°).
3. The diagonals BISECT each other at RIGHT ANGLES (90°).
4. The diagonals split the rhombus into 4 congruent right triangles.

Formulas:
  Area = (1/2) × d₁ × d₂
  Side length s = √[(d₁/2)² + (d₂/2)²]
  Perimeter = 4s

Example: A rhombus has diagonals of lengths 12 cm and 16 cm. Find its area and side length.
  Area = (1/2) × 12 × 16 = 96 cm².
  Half-diagonals: 6 cm and 8 cm.
  Right triangle legs: 6 and 8  =>  hypotenuse (side s) = √(6² + 8²) = 10 cm (from 3-4-5 triple).

Tip: The 4 right triangles formed by the diagonals are key to solving almost any rhombus problem with the Pythagorean Theorem!
