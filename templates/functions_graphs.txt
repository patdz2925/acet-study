Graph transformations and analyzing functional relationships.

Transformations of f(x):
1. Vertical Shift:
   - f(x) + c moves UP by c units.
   - f(x) - c moves DOWN by c units.
2. Horizontal Shift:
   - f(x - c) moves RIGHT by c units.
   - f(x + c) moves LEFT by c units.
3. Reflections:
   - -f(x) reflects across the x-axis.
   - f(-x) reflects across the y-axis.

Example: If the graph of y = x² is shifted 3 units right and 2 units down, what is its new equation?
  Shift right 3: (x - 3)²
  Shift down 2: (x - 3)² - 2
  New equation: y = (x - 3)² - 2.

Tip: Watch out for horizontal shifts: f(x - 3) moves in the POSITIVE x direction (right), not left!
