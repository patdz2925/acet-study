Circle inscribed in a square (or square inscribed in a circle) geometry problems.

Case 1: Circle INSCRIBED in a Square of side s:
  - Diameter of circle = Side of square (d = s, r = s/2).
  - Area of square = s²
  - Area of circle = πr² = π(s/2)² = (π/4)s² ≈ 0.785s²
  - Shaded area (corners) = s² - π(s/2)².

Case 2: Square INSCRIBED in a Circle of radius r:
  - Diagonal of square = Diameter of circle = 2r.
  - Side of square = (2r) / √2 = r√2.
  - Area of square = (r√2)² = 2r².

Example: A circle is inscribed in a square of side 10. What is the area of the circle?
  Diameter = 10  =>  Radius r = 5.
  Area = π(5)² = 25π.

Tip: Draw a quick sketch! Check whether the circle is INSIDE the square (d = side) or OUTSIDE the square (d = diagonal).
