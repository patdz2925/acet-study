Exponent rules govern the simplification and combination of powers.

Essential Laws of Exponents:
1. Product Rule: xᵃ · xᵇ = x^(a + b)
2. Quotient Rule: xᵃ / xᵇ = x^(a - b)
3. Power of a Power: (xᵃ)ᵇ = x^(ab)
4. Power of a Product: (xy)ᵃ = xᵃyᵃ
5. Zero Exponent: x⁰ = 1 (for x ≠ 0)
6. Negative Exponent: x^(-a) = 1 / xᵃ
7. Fractional Exponent (Radicals): x^(m/n) = ⁿ√(xᵐ) = (ⁿ√x)ᵐ

Example: Simplify (2x³y²)³ / (4x⁴y)
  Numerator: 2³ · (x³)³ · (y²)³ = 8x⁹y⁶
  Denominator: 4x⁴y¹
  Simplify: (8/4) · x^(9 - 4) · y^(6 - 1) = 2x⁵y⁵.

Tip: Add exponents when MULTIPLYING common bases; multiply exponents when RAISING a power to another power!
