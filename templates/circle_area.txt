Circle area and perimeter (circumference) formulas are standard ACET geometry staples.

Core Formulas:
  Area = πr²
  Circumference = 2πr = πd
  Diameter = 2r

Sectors and Arcs (for central angle θ in degrees):
  Arc Length = (θ / 360°) × 2πr
  Sector Area = (θ / 360°) × πr²

Example: If the circumference of a circle is 10π, what is its area?
  2πr = 10π  =>  r = 5
  Area = πr² = π(5)² = 25π.

Tip: Pay attention to whether the problem provides the diameter or the radius. Forgetting to divide the diameter by 2 is the #1 mistake!
