Geometry and area calculations for trapezoids.

Formulas:
1. Area = [(b₁ + b₂) / 2] × h
   Where b₁, b₂ are the two parallel bases and h is the perpendicular height.
2. Median (Mid-segment) of a Trapezoid:
   m = (b₁ + b₂) / 2
   Therefore: Area = Median × Height.

Isosceles Trapezoid Properties:
- Non-parallel sides (legs) are equal in length.
- Base angles are equal.
- Diagonals are equal in length.
- Dropping heights from top corners splits the trapezoid into a rectangle and two congruent right triangles.

Example: A trapezoid has parallel bases of 8 cm and 14 cm and a height of 6 cm. Find its area.
  Area = [(8 + 14) / 2] × 6 = [22 / 2] × 6 = 11 × 6 = 66 cm².

Tip: The height must be perpendicular to both bases. Do not use the length of the slanted non-parallel sides for height!
