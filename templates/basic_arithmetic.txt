Basic arithmetic involves mastery of PEMDAS/BODMAS, sign rules, and arithmetic efficiency.

Order of Operations (PEMDAS):
1. Parentheses / Brackets
2. Exponents / Roots
3. Multiplication and Division (from left to right!)
4. Addition and Subtraction (from left to right!)

Sign Rules:
- Negative × Negative = Positive
- Negative × Positive = Negative
- Subtracting a negative: a - (-b) = a + b

Example: Evaluate: 18 - 3 × (4 - 6)² ÷ 2
  1. Parentheses: (4 - 6) = -2
  2. Exponent: (-2)² = 4
  3. Expression is now: 18 - 3 × 4 ÷ 2
  4. Multiply/Divide left to right: 3 × 4 = 12; 12 ÷ 2 = 6
  5. Subtract: 18 - 6 = 12.

Tip: Do multiplication and division strictly left-to-right as they appear. Multiplication does NOT take priority over division.
