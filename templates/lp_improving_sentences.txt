Improving Sentences tests sentence structure, clarity, and conciseness.

Key Rules & Strategy:
1. Look for conciseness: The most effective sentence conveys the complete thought with zero redundant words.
2. Eliminate run-on sentences and comma splices (connect independent clauses with semicolons or conjunctions).
3. Fix awkward phrasing and passive voice constructions when active voice is clearer.
4. Ensure parallel structure in compound verbs, prepositional phrases, and series.

Example: "Due to the fact that it was raining, therefore the game was postponed."
  Improvement: "Because it was raining, the game was postponed." (Removes redundant "therefore" and bloated "due to the fact that").

Tip: Beware of choices that sound overly formal but introduce grammatical awkwardness. Simpler, grammatically tight sentences are almost always correct.
