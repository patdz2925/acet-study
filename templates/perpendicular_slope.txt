Slope relationships for parallel and perpendicular lines.

Rules:
1. Parallel Lines have IDENTICAL slopes:
   m₁ = m₂
2. Perpendicular Lines have NEGATIVE RECIPROCAL slopes:
   m₁ · m₂ = -1  =>  m₂ = -1 / m₁
3. Special Cases:
   - Horizontal line (m = 0) is perpendicular to a vertical line (undefined slope).

Example: Find the equation of the line perpendicular to y = -(3/4)x + 2 that passes through (0, 0).
  Original slope m₁ = -3/4.
  Perpendicular slope m₂ = -(-4/3) = +4/3.
  Through origin (0, 0): y = (4/3)x.

Tip: To find a perpendicular slope, remember two steps: flip the fraction upside down AND change the positive/negative sign!
