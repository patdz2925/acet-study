Capital-to-Country analogies test geographical knowledge and place relationships.

Relationship: [CITY] is the official capital of [NATION].

Strategy:
1. Form the bridge: "[A] is the capital city of country [B]."
2. Verify orientation: Make sure the capital is on the left and the country on the right (or vice versa).
3. Do not confuse largest cities with capital cities (e.g., Sydney is NOT the capital of Australia; Canberra is).

Example: MANILA : PHILIPPINES
  Bridge: Manila is the capital of the Philippines.
  Match: TOKYO : JAPAN (Tokyo is the capital of Japan).

Tip: ACET often tests well-known world capitals (Paris:France, Rome:Italy, Madrid:Spain, Beijing:China, London:United Kingdom).
