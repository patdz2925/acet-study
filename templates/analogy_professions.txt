Profession analogies relate a professional to their tool, workplace, or medium.

Relationship: A [PROFESSIONAL] works with [TOOL / MEDIUM / WORKPLACE].

Strategy:
1. Build the specific bridge: "A [PROFESSION] works in a [WORKPLACE]" or "uses a [TOOL] to perform work."
2. Check specificity:
   - Carpenter : Wood (Worker : Raw Material).
   - Surgeon : Scalpel (Worker : Characteristic Tool).
   - Chef : Kitchen (Worker : Workplace).

Example: SURGEON : SCALPEL
  Bridge: A surgeon uses a scalpel as a primary instrument of work.
  Match: AUTHOR : PEN (or PAINTER : BRUSH).

Tip: Distinguish between the worker's tool, raw material, and finished product. All options might relate to professions, but only one will match the exact role.
