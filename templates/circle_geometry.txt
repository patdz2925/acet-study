Circle geometry theorems deal with chords, inscribed angles, and tangents.

Key Theorems:
1. Inscribed Angle Theorem: An angle inscribed in a circle is HALF the measure of its intercepted central arc:
   Inscribed Angle = (1/2) × Central Angle.
2. Thales's Theorem: An angle inscribed in a semicircle (subtended by the diameter) is ALWAYS 90° (a right angle).
3. Tangent-Radius Theorem: A tangent is perpendicular to the radius at the point of tangency.
4. Two Tangents from an External Point: Tangent segments from the same external point to a circle are equal in length.

Example: Triangle ABC is inscribed in a circle where AC is the diameter. What is angle B?
  By Thales's Theorem, angle B = 90°.

Tip: When a diameter is part of an inscribed triangle, you immediately have a right triangle—you can use the Pythagorean Theorem!
