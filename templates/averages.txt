An average (mean) is the sum of values divided by the count.

Key formulas:
1. Average = Sum / Count
2. Sum = Average × Count
3. Count = Sum / Average

Example: The average of 5 numbers is 12.
  Sum = 12 × 5 = 60

If you know the average and any two of the three values (sum, count, average), you can find the third.
