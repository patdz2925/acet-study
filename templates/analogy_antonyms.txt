Analogy Antonyms compare word pairs that express opposite meanings.

Relationship: [WORD A] is the exact opposite of [WORD B].

Strategy:
1. State the relationship: "A is the opposite of B."
2. Determine degree of intensity:
   - If A and B are absolute opposites (e.g., Dead : Alive), the answer must also be absolute.
   - If they are graded opposites (e.g., Chilly : Warm), the answer should reflect a similar scale.
3. Check parts of speech: Adjective : Adjective, Noun : Noun, Verb : Verb.

Example: GARRULOUS : TACITURN (Extremely talkative : Habitually silent)
  Bridge: Garrulous is the antonym of taciturn.
  Match: PRODIGAL : FRUGAL (Wasteful/extravagant : Economical/sparing).

Tip: Double-check that the relationship is opposite, not just loosely contrasting.
