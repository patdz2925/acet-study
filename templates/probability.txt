Probability measures the likelihood of an event occurring.

Core Formula:
  P(Event) = (Number of favorable outcomes) / (Total number of possible outcomes)
  0 ≤ P(E) ≤ 1

Key Rules:
1. Complement Rule: P(Not E) = 1 - P(E)
2. Independent Events (Both A and B occur): P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B)
3. Mutually Exclusive Events (Either A or B occurs): P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B)

Example: A bag has 4 red, 6 blue, and 5 green marbles (total = 15).
  P(Blue) = 6 / 15 = 2 / 5.
  P(Not Red) = 1 - (4 / 15) = 11 / 15.

Tip: For "at least one" problems, compute 1 - P(none). It is usually much faster than adding multiple cases!
