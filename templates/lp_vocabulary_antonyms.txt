Antonym questions test your ability to determine the exact opposite meaning of a word.

Key Rules & Strategy:
1. Determine the root, prefix, and suffix of the target word:
   - "in-", "un-", "dis-" (not); "bene-" (good); "mal-" (bad).
2. Establish the connotation: Is the target word positive (+), negative (-), or neutral (0)?
3. If the target is negative, its antonym must be positive.
4. Beware of the Trap: The synonym is almost always included in the choices to fool hasty test-takers!

Example: EPHEMERAL (short-lived, fleeting)
  Opposite: PERMANENT, ENDURING, EVERLASTING.
  Trap: "Brief" and "Transient" are synonyms, not antonyms.

Tip: Mentally define the target word in a short sentence first, negate that sentence, and then match the negated meaning to the choices.
