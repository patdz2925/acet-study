Detail Recall questions test your ability to locate and retrieve specific factual information directly stated in the text.

Key Strategy:
1. Re-read the question stem and highlight the specific key terms or names requested.
2. Scan the passage for those keywords, synonyms, or paraphrases.
3. Read the surrounding 2-3 lines carefully.
4. The correct choice is almost always a close paraphrase of a specific sentence in the text.
5. Beware of distortions: Distractor options often swap dates, reverse relationships, or exaggerate statements.

Tip: If a choice introduces information not mentioned anywhere in the passage, eliminate it immediately, even if it is factually true in real life.
