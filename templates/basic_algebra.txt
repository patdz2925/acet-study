Basic algebra involves solving for an unknown variable.

To solve linear equations:
1. Isolate the variable on one side of the equation.
2. Do the same operation to both sides (add, subtract, multiply, divide).
3. Simplify step by step.

Example: Solve 2x + 3 = 11
  2x = 11 - 3    (subtract 3 from both sides)
  2x = 8        (simplify)
  x = 4         (divide both sides by 2)

Always substitute your answer back into the original equation to verify.
