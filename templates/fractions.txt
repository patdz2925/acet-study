A fraction represents part of a whole.

To solve fraction problems:
1. To find a fraction of a number: multiply the number by the fraction.
   Example: 3/4 of 80 = 80 × (3/4) = 60
2. To multiply fractions: multiply numerators, multiply denominators.
3. To simplify: divide both by their greatest common divisor.

Always double-check: does your answer make sense relative to the whole?
