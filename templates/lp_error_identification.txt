Error Identification tests your grasp of standard English grammar and usage.

Key Rules & Strategy:
1. Scan the sentence for Subject-Verb Agreement (singular subjects need singular verbs).
2. Check Pronoun-Antecedent Agreement and Pronoun Case (subjective vs. objective, e.g., "he and I" vs "him and me").
3. Inspect Verb Tense consistency across related clauses.
4. Look for Faulty Parallelism in lists or comparisons (e.g., "singing, dancing, and to act" -> "acting").
5. Watch out for Dangling or Misplaced Modifiers.

Example: "Neither the teacher nor the students was present."
  Error: In "neither... nor", the verb agrees with the closer subject ("students"), so it should be "were present".

Tip: Read the sentence silently as a whole. Don't correct what isn't wrong; if no option contains an error, look for "No Error" if available.
