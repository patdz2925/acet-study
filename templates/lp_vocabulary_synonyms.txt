Synonym questions test your understanding of words in precision and context.

Key Rules & Strategy:
1. Recall the primary meaning and secondary connotations of the word.
2. Break down unfamiliar words using Greek/Latin roots, prefixes, and suffixes.
3. Eliminate choices with opposite connotations (positive vs. negative).
4. Substitute each option into a sample phrase to test whether it preserves the nuance.

Example: METICULOUS (showing great attention to detail; very careful and precise)
  Synonym: PAINSTAKING, FASTIDIOUS, THOROUGH.
  Distractor: "Hasty" (antonym) or "Generous" (unrelated positive word).

Tip: If two choices are exact synonyms of each other, neither is likely the correct answer unless only one matches the target word.
