Ratios compare two quantities.

To solve ratio problems:
1. Identify the two quantities being compared.
2. Set up a proportion if finding an unknown: a/b = c/d
3. Cross-multiply: a × d = b × c
4. Solve for the unknown.

Example: If 3 notebooks cost $4.50, then 7 notebooks cost x.
  3/4.50 = 7/x
  3x = 31.50
  x = 10.50

Remember to keep units consistent and always check if your answer makes sense.
