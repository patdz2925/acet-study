Function analogies test the relationship between an object/tool and its primary purpose.

Relationship: [TOOL / OBJECT] is specifically designed to [FUNCTION / ACTION].

Strategy:
1. Bridge sentence: "The primary purpose of [A] is to [B]."
2. Focus on PRIMARY function, not incidental uses:
   - A knife is designed to cut (not just to spread butter).
   - A ruler is designed to measure length.
3. Ensure the direction of the bridge matches (Tool : Function vs. Function : Tool).

Example: SCISSORS : CUT
  Bridge: Scissors are an instrument used to cut.
  Match: PEN : WRITE or THERMOMETER : MEASURE (A pen is used to write).

Tip: Verify that the tool performs the action directly rather than merely being present during the action.
