Definition Match analogies test words where one term is the dictionary definition or defining quality of the other.

Relationship: By definition, [A] is characterized by [B].

Strategy:
1. Bridge sentence: "A person or thing that is [A] inherently exhibits [B]."
2. Look for essential, non-accidental qualities:
   - A martyr is inherently willing to sacrifice.
   - A novice is inherently lacking in experience.

Example: PHILANTHROPIST : BENEVOLENCE
  Bridge: A philanthropist is defined by benevolence.
  Match: SKEPTIC : DOUBT (A skeptic is defined by doubt).

Tip: The relationship must be true by definition, not just by coincidence or common occurrence.
