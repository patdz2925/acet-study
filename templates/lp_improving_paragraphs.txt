Improving Paragraphs evaluates organization, unity, and cohesion in writing.

Key Rules & Strategy:
1. Identify the topic sentence and main idea of the paragraph.
2. Check for logical flow: Look for transitional words (furthermore, however, consequently, in contrast).
3. Eliminate off-topic sentences that disrupt the paragraph's unity.
4. When asked where to insert a new sentence, identify the sentence it connects to chronologically or conceptually.

Example: If Sentence 1 introduces a theory and Sentence 3 gives results, a sentence describing the experiment belongs between Sentences 1 and 2 or 2 and 3.

Tip: Pay close attention to pronoun references (e.g., "this discovery", "these results")—they must immediately follow the noun they refer to.
