Linear equations represent straight-line relationships with variables to the first power.

Forms of a Line:
1. Slope-Intercept Form: y = mx + b
   m = slope = (y₂ - y₁) / (x₂ - x₁), b = y-intercept.
2. Point-Slope Form: y - y₁ = m(x - x₁)
3. Standard Form: Ax + By = C

Slope Interpretation:
- Positive slope (m > 0): rises from left to right.
- Negative slope (m < 0): falls from left to right.
- Zero slope (m = 0): horizontal line (y = c).
- Undefined slope: vertical line (x = c).

Example: Find the slope of the line passing through (2, 5) and (6, 13).
  m = (13 - 5) / (6 - 2) = 8 / 4 = 2.

Tip: Be careful with negative coordinates when calculating slope: y₂ - y₁ requires subtracting negative numbers correctly.
