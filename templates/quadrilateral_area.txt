Formulas for the areas of various quadrilaterals.

Core Area Formulas:
1. Rectangle: Area = base × height = l × w
2. Square: Area = s² = (1/2)d² (where d is diagonal)
3. Parallelogram: Area = base × perpendicular height = b × h
4. Rhombus: Area = (1/2) × d₁ × d₂ (where d₁, d₂ are diagonals)
5. Trapezoid: Area = [(b₁ + b₂) / 2] × h

Example: A parallelogram has a base of 12 cm and a slant side of 8 cm with an altitude (height) of 5 cm. What is its area?
  Area = base × perpendicular height = 12 × 5 = 60 cm².
  (The slant side of 8 cm is unnecessary information).

Tip: Always use the PERPENDICULAR height, never the slant side length!
