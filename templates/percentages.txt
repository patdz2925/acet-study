Percentages express a part per hundred.

To solve percentage problems:
1. Percent increase: ((new - old) / old) × 100
2. Percent of a number: (percent / 100) × number
3. Find the original: if X% of Y = Z, then Y = Z / (X/100)

Example: A store sold 200 items in January and 250 in February.
  Percent increase = ((250 - 200) / 200) × 100 = 25%

Always identify the base (the "of" number) carefully.
