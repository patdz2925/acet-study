Category analogies test the relationship between a specific item and its general class (Item : Class).

Relationship: [ITEM] is a specific type / member of [CATEGORY].

Strategy:
1. Formulate: "An [A] is a type/member of [B]."
2. Notice the order: Is it Specific : General or General : Specific?
3. Avoid loose associations: A whale is a mammal (classification), not just something found in the ocean.

Example: TROUT : FISH
  Bridge: A trout is a specific kind of fish.
  Match: OAK : TREE (An oak is a specific kind of tree).

Tip: Beware of "part-to-whole" vs "category" confusion: A branch is a PART of a tree, but an oak IS A tree.
