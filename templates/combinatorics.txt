Combinatorics covers counting techniques, permutations, and combinations.

Key Formulas:
1. Fundamental Counting Principle: If task 1 has m ways and task 2 has n ways, together there are m × n ways.
2. Permutation (Order Matters):
   nPr = n! / (n - r)!
   Example: First, second, third place; arrangements in a line.
3. Combination (Order Does NOT Matter):
   nCr = n! / [r! × (n - r)!]
   Example: Committees, selecting a group of friends, hands of cards.

Example: How many ways to choose a committee of 3 from 7 people?
  nCr = 7C3 = (7 × 6 × 5) / (3 × 2 × 1) = 35 ways.

Tip: Always ask first: "Does changing the order create a different outcome?" If yes -> Permutation. If no -> Combination.
