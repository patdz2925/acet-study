Paragraph Arrangement requires ordering scrambled sentences into a coherent text.

Key Rules & Strategy:
1. Find the Opening Sentence: It introduces the topic independently and rarely begins with pronouns (he, it, they) or contrast transitions (however, therefore).
2. Spot Cohesive Pairs: Group sentences that refer to each other (e.g., a term defined in sentence A and elaborated in sentence B).
3. Follow Chronological or Cause-and-Effect order: Background -> Problem -> Solution -> Conclusion.
4. Eliminate choices that place concluding sentences early.

Example:
  (1) Furthermore, trees reduce urban heat.
  (2) Urban green spaces provide vital benefits.
  (3) For instance, parks promote mental well-being.
  Order: 2 (general topic) -> 3 (first benefit) -> 1 (additional benefit with transition).

Tip: Identify the first sentence, then check the answer choices. Often, only one or two choices start with the correct opening sentence.
