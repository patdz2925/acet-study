Author's Purpose questions ask WHY the passage was written.

Key Categories of Purpose:
- To Inform / Explain: Objective presentation of facts, data, or phenomena (neutral tone).
- To Persuade / Argue: Advocating a specific stance, policy, or interpretation (assertive or critical tone).
- To Describe / Portray: Vivid depiction of a person, event, or scene.
- To Criticize / Refute: Pointing out flaws or weaknesses in an existing view.

Strategy:
1. Pay attention to the concluding paragraph: Authors frequently state their central recommendation or stance there.
2. Watch tone indicators: Words like "unfortunately", "promising", "must", or "alleged" reveal the author's attitude.
3. Eliminate overly broad or overly narrow choices.

Tip: Watch for strong extreme verbs in choices (e.g., "prove", "denounce")—unless the author is truly radical, moderate verbs ("discuss", "examine", "argue") are often correct.
